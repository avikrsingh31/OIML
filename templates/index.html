GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

Department of Consumer Affairs — Legal Metrology Division

THE LEGAL METROLOGY ACT, 2009

The Legal Metrology Division under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution regulates the manufacturing, sale, and calibration of weighing and measuring instruments across Indian territory. This automated smart portal enables field enforcement officers to perform computerized compliance tests (Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments - NAWI), verify observed deviations against Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) thresholds, and issue instantaneous digitally sealed certificates.
